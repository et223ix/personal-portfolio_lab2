About me

My name is Elin Tengelin, and I grew up in a city called Örebro. One of my favorite memories from my childhood would probably be playing shoe shop with my older brother at my grandma and grandad's house. My grandma used to work in a shoe shop for many years and had tons of shoe boxes and shoes that we used to play with. I remember that she had a pair of blue high-heeled shoes from the 1950s that I loved and always used to put on.

My personal interests

As a person I am very passionate about creating things and expressing ideas through graphic designs, arts and makeup looks. I think what got me into this passion of mine is my family who also loves to create things such as music, art and handmade crafts. The creating part have always been a way for me to relax and recharge, allowing me to enter a state of quiet motion where my thoughts can run freely. Another interest of mine is travelling. I really enjoy exploring new places and experiencing different cultures. My interest in travelling began with my first trip at the age of 14. Since then, I have become interested in discovering new places, learning about different cultures, and gaining new perspectives.
