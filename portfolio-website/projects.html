Chair assignment created in Maya 3D

I created this chair two years ago as part of a graphic design course. The assignment was to create a complete 3D model of a chair from scratch using a 3D program called Maya. I started by creating and shaping the individual parts of the chair and then assembled them together to create the final model. I also worked on the materials, textures, lightning and shadows to make the chair look more realistic.
